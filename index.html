38-hole spinner • Weight inventory & correction worksheet

ENGINEERING REVIEW REQUIRED — NOT APPROVED FOR MAINTENANCE. This prototype uses the supplied photographed figures. Weight inventory and vector arithmetic are implemented; the RPM/vibration table estimator is not an exact AMM lookup and cannot establish an approved correction. Verify configuration, effectivity, procedure, and all values in current controlled GE/MD-11 maintenance data before any installation.

1 • Measured vibration

Hole location tables are in Figures 525 (88–97%) and 526 (98–106%). Select the location from the controlled manual and enter it here. The app does not infer a hole from an unvalidated transcription.

2 • Change a screw at the selected hole

Records the installed screw and computes its effective weight difference against the currently installed screw. No installation action is authorized by this worksheet.
